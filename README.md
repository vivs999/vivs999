# Vivaan Rochlani

Computational biology, Monte Carlo simulation, and a Mac utility.

## Projects

**[marine-mt-structure](https://github.com/vivs999/marine-mt-structure)**
Predicts the habitat of 145 marine animal species from one protein, metallothionein, using 532 Boltz-1 zinc-bound predicted structures and ESM-2 sequence embeddings (0.870 accuracy against a 0.633 majority-class baseline). A phylogenetic correction (PGLS) roughly halves the polar effect without erasing it.
<br>Python · ESM-2 · Boltz-1 · scikit-learn

**[Senior-Solar-Sprint-Simulations](https://github.com/vivs999/Senior-Solar-Sprint-Simulations)**
Monte Carlo simulation of a solar car, parallelized with OpenMP, modeling motor torque, drag and friction. It set the car's gearing and predicted the real car within 10%; the car placed 4th of 16 in Senior Solar Sprint at the 2025 TSA National Leadership Conference.
<br>Fortran · OpenMP

**[DS4Mouse](https://github.com/vivs999/DS4Mouse)**
A native Mac menu bar app that turns a DualShock 4 touchpad into a mouse, with remappable gestures and buttons.
<br>Swift · IOKit · CoreGraphics
